My journey with Kathak has been one of continuous learning, practice and discovery. With more than a decade devoted to this art form, I continue to learn under the guidance of Guru Smt. Swati Sinha ji.

A trained and certified Kathak dancer, performer and teacher, I find joy not only in practising and performing but also in sharing what I learn along the way. Through teaching, artistic collaborations and exploring different forms of creative expression, I hope to discover new perspectives while staying connected to the traditions that shaped me.

For me, dance is an ever-evolving journey — one of learning, connecting and growing.

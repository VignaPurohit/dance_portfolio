My journey with Kathak has been anything but a straight line. What began as an introduction to this art form has grown into a journey of learning, rediscovery and finding my way back to dance. Over nearly ten years of training and practice, Kathak has continued to find its place in my life, shaping the dancer and artist I am today.

I continue to learn under the guidance of Guru Smt. Swati Sinha ji. As a trained Kathak dancer, performer and teacher, I find joy in both practising and sharing this art. Through performances, teaching and artistic collaborations, I continue to explore new perspectives while staying connected to the traditions that have shaped me.

For me, dance is an ever-evolving journey — one of learning, connecting and growing.
